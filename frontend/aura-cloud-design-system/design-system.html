Flow field

WebGL fragment shader: 45 light strands fanning out from the left edge. Intro value tweens 0 → 1 over 3.5s (power2.inOut) so strands grow in; strands bend and ripple toward the pointer. Ambient cyan/teal glows (blur 140px) sit above the canvas. In the Overview the canvas also scrubs y 35% and scale 1.22 across the page scroll.
